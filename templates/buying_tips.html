5-Point Freshness Check

  • Firmness: Press gently — fresh vegetables feel firm and spring back. Soft spots mean breakdown has begun.
  • Colour: Vibrant, deep colour = high anthocyanin/chlorophyll. Dull or yellowing = ageing and nutrient loss.
  • Fragrance: Fresh tomatoes smell earthy and slightly sweet. Odourless or sour smell = stored too long.
  • Cut surfaces: If buying cut vegetables, the flesh should be moist and not dried out or brown.
  • Stems and leaves: Leafy greens should have crisp, upright leaves. Drooping = moisture loss of 15–25%.

The Right and Wrong Way to Store Onions & Potatoes
Improper storage causes 30–40% of household produce waste in India. Onions stored incorrectly go soft within 3–5 days; stored correctly they last 2–3 months.

Storage Guide by Vegetable

🧅
Onions
Cool, dry, well-ventilated space. Mesh bag or hanging net. Never refrigerate — moisture softens them. Keep away from potatoes (ethylene gas speeds spoilage).
✓ Shelf life: 2–3 months
🥔
Potatoes
Dark, cool area (10–15°C). Avoid light — triggers solanine (toxic green pigment). Paper bags or cardboard boxes. Never plastic bags.
✓ Shelf life: 3–4 months
🍅
Tomatoes
Room temperature until ripe. Never refrigerate unripe tomatoes — kills flavour compounds. Once cut, refrigerate and use within 2 days.
✓ Shelf life: 5–7 days at room temp
🥬
Leafy Greens
Wrap in a slightly damp cloth, place in fridge. Or stand in a glass of water like flowers. Wash only just before use, not before storing.
✓ Shelf life: 4–5 days in fridge
⚠ Common Storage Mistakes to Avoid

Never store onions and potatoes together — they release gases that accelerate each other's spoilage. Never put whole tomatoes in the fridge — temperatures below 13°C destroy the enzymes responsible for flavour and aroma. Plastic bags trap moisture and cause mould in most root vegetables.

✅ How to Identify Genuine Organic Produce
  • Look for PGS-India (Participatory Guarantee System) or NPOP certification badge
  • Organic produce often has slight imperfections — natural, not cosmetically perfect
  • AgroHub farmer profiles show certification status for organic listings
  • Ask for third-party soil test results — genuine organic farmers have them

⚠ Why Avoid Off-Season Produce?

Off-season produce is either greenhouse grown (higher pesticide use, lower vitamin content) or cold-stored for 2–6 months (enzymatic degradation reduces nutrients by 15–40%). Seasonal produce is harvested at peak ripeness and reaches you within 24–48 hours on AgroHub.
