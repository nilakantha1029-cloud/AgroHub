Platform Overview

AgroHub is a digital agricultural supply chain platform developed to bridge the gap between Farmers, Customers/Dealers, and Service Providers. The platform enables:

🌾
Farmers can list and sell agricultural produce such as crops, rice, wheat, vegetables, fruits, and other farm goods. They can also book transport vehicles, rent farming equipment, and reserve storage facilities through the platform.
🛒
Customers & Dealers can directly browse, select, and purchase agricultural goods from farmers with complete transparency in pricing and quality.
🚛
Service Providers can offer transport vehicles, farming equipment (tractors, harvesters, cultivators), and storage facilities for booking by farmers.

By registering and using this platform, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age, legally capable of entering into agreements, and agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions.

User Roles & Responsibilities

👨‍🌾

Farmers

  • Farmers may register on the platform to list agricultural produce including crops, rice, wheat, vegetables, fruits, and other farm goods for direct sale.
  • Farmers must provide accurate and honest product details including product name, variety, quantity, quality grade, price per unit, and harvest date.
  • Farmers are responsible for ensuring that listed goods are genuinely available and can be delivered or picked up as specified.
  • Farmers may book transport vehicles from service providers to transport their harvested goods to buyers or storage locations.
  • Farmers may rent farming equipment such as tractors, harvesters, cultivators, tillers, and other agricultural machinery from service providers for a specified duration.
  • Farmers may reserve storage facilities offered by service providers for specific dates and durations to safely store their produce before sale.
  • Farmers must comply with local agricultural and trading laws when listing and selling produce.
  • Farmers shall not list goods that are spoiled, contaminated, or misrepresented in terms of quality or quantity.
🛒

Customers & Dealers

  • Customers and dealers may browse the platform's marketplace to view and purchase agricultural goods directly from farmers.
  • Buyers are responsible for reviewing product listings carefully including price, quantity, quality, and pickup or delivery options before placing an order.
  • Customers must ensure their payment details are accurate and that sufficient funds are available at the time of purchase.
  • Buyers may not resell platform-listed goods under false pretenses or misrepresent their origin.
  • Customers must raise any disputes or complaints through the platform's official support channel within 48 hours of receiving goods.
  • Customers agree not to engage in fraudulent behavior such as claiming non-delivery of goods that were actually received.
🚛

Service Providers

  • Service providers may list transport vehicles, farming equipment, and storage facilities available for booking by farmers.
  • Providers must ensure all listed services are accurately described with correct pricing, availability, specifications, and service area coverage.
  • Providers offering transport vehicles must ensure vehicles are road-worthy, properly registered, insured, and operated by licensed drivers.
  • Providers offering farming equipment such as tractors, harvesters, cultivators, and tillers must ensure all machinery is properly maintained, safe to operate, and delivered or available at the agreed location and time.
  • Providers offering storage facilities must ensure warehouses or storage units are clean, pest-free, secure, and appropriate for storing agricultural produce for the booked duration.
  • Providers must honor confirmed bookings and must inform farmers immediately if any service cancellation or delay is unavoidable.
  • Providers are responsible for any damage caused to farmer goods due to negligent transport, improper equipment, or inadequate storage conditions.

Crop & Agricultural Goods Marketplace

The AgroHub marketplace enables direct trade between farmers and buyers without unnecessary middlemen, ensuring fair pricing and transparency.

  • Farmers may list any legal agricultural goods including crops (rice, wheat, corn, pulses), vegetables, fruits, spices, dairy products, and other farm produce.
  • Each listing must include: product name, variety/type, quantity available, unit price, quality grade (A/B/C), harvest date, location, and pickup or delivery options.
  • Product images may be uploaded to help buyers assess quality. Images must be genuine and accurately represent the listed product.
  • Buyers can filter listings by product type, location, price range, and quality grade to find the best matches for their needs.
  • Once an order is confirmed, the farmer is obligated to fulfill it. Failure to fulfill confirmed orders without valid reason may result in account penalties.
  • The platform does not physically hold or transfer goods. All physical transactions are between the farmer and buyer directly.
  • The platform reserves the right to remove any listing that is fraudulent, misleading, or violates food safety or agricultural regulations.

Transport Vehicle Booking

Farmers can book transport vehicles from registered service providers to move their agricultural goods from farms to markets, buyers, or storage facilities.

  • Transport vehicles available include trucks, mini-trucks, tractors with trailers, refrigerated vans (for perishables), and other agricultural transport vehicles.
  • Farmers must provide accurate pickup location, delivery destination, cargo details (type of goods, weight, volume), and preferred date and time when booking.
  • Service providers are responsible for ensuring the vehicle arrives on time, is appropriate for the cargo, and the driver is licensed and professional.
  • Farmers must ensure goods are properly packed and ready for loading at the agreed pickup time. Delays caused by the farmer may result in additional charges from the provider.
  • The platform does not guarantee the availability of vehicles on short notice. It is recommended to book at least 24-48 hours in advance.
  • In case of vehicle breakdown or unavailability, the service provider must inform the farmer immediately and either arrange an alternative vehicle or process a full refund.
  • Any damage to goods during transport due to provider negligence must be reported within 24 hours of delivery, and the provider shall be held accountable as per the agreed terms.
  • The platform is not directly responsible for the physical transportation of goods but will mediate disputes between farmers and transport providers.
05

Farming Equipment Rental

Farmers can rent modern farming machinery from service providers on the platform, including tractors, harvesters, cultivators, tillers, seeders, sprayers, and other agricultural equipment.

  • Equipment listings must include: equipment type, model/brand, rental price (hourly/daily), fuel cost terms (included or separate), service area, and availability calendar.
  • Farmers must specify the exact equipment required, rental start date, duration, and purpose of use when making a booking.
  • Service providers must deliver or make available the equipment in fully operational and safe condition at the agreed time and location.
  • Farmers are responsible for operating equipment carefully and in accordance with standard agricultural practices. Misuse, reckless operation, or damage caused by the farmer will result in the farmer bearing repair costs.
  • Normal wear and tear during proper use is the responsibility of the service provider. However, any major damage or breakdown caused by improper use shall be investigated and resolved through the platform's dispute resolution system.
  • Farmers must return equipment at the agreed time. Extended usage beyond the booked period will attract additional charges as per the provider's rates.
  • Any breakdown during proper use should be reported to the provider immediately. The provider must send a replacement or repair the equipment within a reasonable time.
  • Fuel costs, operator charges (if applicable), and transportation of equipment to and from the farm must be clearly agreed upon before booking confirmation.

Storage Facility Booking

Farmers can book storage facilities from service providers to safely store their agricultural produce for a specified number of days before it is sold or transported.

  • Storage facilities listed on the platform may include: open warehouses, cold storage units, silos, grain storage rooms, and climate-controlled facilities suitable for different types of produce.
  • Providers must clearly specify: storage type, capacity (in metric tons or cubic feet), temperature control (if available), security arrangements, location, price per day/week/month, and items accepted.
  • Farmers must clearly state the type of produce to be stored, quantity, required temperature conditions, storage start date, and duration when booking.
  • The storage provider is responsible for maintaining appropriate conditions — including temperature, humidity, pest control, and security — throughout the entire booked duration.
  • If stored goods suffer spoilage or damage due to negligence by the storage provider (such as power failure without backup, pest infestation, or security breach), the provider shall be held liable for compensation.
  • Farmers may inspect their stored goods during working hours. Providers must not restrict reasonable access to stored produce.
  • Farmers must retrieve their goods by the end of the booked storage period. Extensions must be booked in advance, subject to availability. Continued storage beyond the booked period without confirmation will incur additional charges.
  • The platform will not be held responsible for losses due to natural disasters, theft by third parties, or events beyond the provider's control, provided the provider has taken all reasonable precautions.

  • Prices displayed are inclusive of any platform fee unless explicitly stated otherwise.
  • Payments for all transactions must be completed through the platform's secure payment gateway. Offline cash transactions arranged outside the platform are done at the user's own risk and the platform bears no responsibility for such transactions.
  • The platform may charge a small service fee or commission on completed transactions, which will be clearly disclosed before payment confirmation.
  • All payment information provided by users is encrypted and securely processed. The platform does not store full card or banking details.
  • Farmers will receive their payment for crop sales after order confirmation and successful delivery to the buyer, as per the payment settlement schedule.
  • Service providers will receive payment for transport, equipment, and storage services after successful service delivery and farmer confirmation.
  • In case of payment disputes, the platform's decision on fund settlement shall be final after due investigation.

Cancellations & Refunds

Crop/Goods Orders

  • Buyers may cancel an order before the farmer has confirmed dispatch for a full refund.
  • After dispatch, cancellations will not be accepted unless the product delivered is significantly different from the listing in terms of quality or quantity.
  • Refunds for eligible cancellations will be processed within 5-7 working days.

Transport Bookings

  • Farmers may cancel a transport booking at least 12 hours before the scheduled pickup for a full refund.
  • Cancellations within 12 hours of pickup may incur a cancellation fee of up to 25% of the booking amount.
  • If the service provider cancels the booking, the farmer is entitled to a full refund.

Equipment Rental Bookings

  • Farmers may cancel an equipment rental booking at least 24 hours before the start time for a full refund.
  • Cancellations within 24 hours may attract a cancellation fee of up to 30% of the rental amount.
  • If equipment arrives in unusable condition, the farmer may refuse and request an immediate refund.

Storage Bookings

  • Farmers may cancel a storage booking at least 48 hours before the storage start date for a full refund.
  • Cancellations within 48 hours will be refunded at 50% of the booking amount.
  • Once storage has begun (goods have been placed), no cancellation refund is applicable for the current period.
  • Early withdrawal of goods before the booked end date does not entitle the farmer to a refund for unused days unless the provider agrees.
